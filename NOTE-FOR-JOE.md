# Joe — how to use the off-market dashboard

**Link:** https://fascinating-jelly-7ea629.netlify.app
(Bookmark it. Works in any browser, nothing to install, no login.)

This is our 442-firm off-market list turned into a "who do we call first, and why"
tool, with the buyer side attached so every target comes with "who would acquire
this." Here's the 2-minute version.

---

## Start here: the 4 tabs
- **Overview** — the lay of the land. Charts for how many firms are high-intent,
  how many owners are retiring/late-career, revenue bands, top states, buyer
  coverage. Skim it once to see the shape of the book.
- **Supply** — the call list. All 442 firms ranked by **seller-intent score**.
  This is where you'll live.
- **Buyers** — our 60 buyers, each with the sellers they best fit.
- **Method** — how the score is built, if you ever want to see the math.

## Your daily routine (do this)
1. Open **Supply**.
2. Click the **🔥 Call-queue** button. That instantly filters to the firms worth
   calling *today*: high/medium intent, we have an email, not yet contacted,
   sorted best-first.
3. Work down the list. Click any firm to open its detail panel.

## Reading a firm (the detail panel)
When you click a firm you get:
- **Why now** — one plain-English line on why this one's timely (e.g. *"Owner ~69,
  at/near retirement — succession window open"*). This is your opener.
- **Score breakdown** — what's driving the intent score (succession signal, owner
  age/tenure, ownership, revenue fit, contactability, warm-path).
- **Succession & ownership facts** — owner count, founder-led vs. multi-partner,
  any retirement/no-successor signal we found.
- **Buyer matches** — the buyers most likely to acquire this firm, with a one-line
  reason each. Use this to frame "we may already have a home for you."
- **Contact + suggested first touch** — the email we have and a starting line.
- **Editable fields** — warm-path + outreach status (see below).

## The single most valuable thing you can do
Fill in **warm-path** on firms where we have a connection — *Joey knows them,
Running Numbers intro, a known buyer relationship, etc.* Type it into the firm's
warm-path field. That field feeds the score, so adding it **re-ranks the firm
upward** — it pulls the people we can actually get to the top of the list. We left
it blank on purpose because it's your knowledge, not something the data could guess.

Also tick the **outreach status** (contacted / call-back / not interested) as you
go, so the call-queue stops showing people you've already worked.

## What the score means (quick)
**Seller-intent 0–100.** High ≥ 70 · Medium 50–69 · Low < 50. Higher = more likely
ready to sell, based mostly on succession/retirement signals and owner age/tenure.
It's a priority cue, not a verdict — your read of the conversation always wins.

## Honest caveats (so you trust it correctly)
- **Service mix & industry niche** are now web-researched for all 441 real firms
  (the lone exception is a placeholder test row). Each researched firm shows its
  actual services, industry specialization, office count, and managing partner,
  pulled from the firm's own website. A handful carry a short "verify" note where
  the source data's location or owner name didn't match the live website — read
  those notes before leaning on the record.
- **Owner ages/tenure** are filled only where the source data stated them; many are
  blank or estimated. The web pass did **not** recompute the seller-intent scores —
  those still reflect the original succession/age signals, so a firm's niche is now
  richer than its score "knows."
- **Edits save in your browser only** for now — if you switch computers, your
  warm-path notes won't follow yet. We can turn on shared/synced editing when we're
  ready; just say the word.
